Hello everyone — this one is a big one. Version 1.7 is all about connection.

In this update, we're adding **Apple Calendar integration** to the app — a seamless experience for people who use Apple Calendar all the time. Connect your calendars and your existing events surface right beneath your plan in My Day, so your plan and your real life finally share one timeline. You choose exactly which calendars flow in, straight from Settings.

And the one I'm most excited about: **real-time sync between the web app and the app is now officially available**. Change something on your phone and watch it land on the web — and the other way around — for a more connected experience across your devices. Even edits you make offline catch up the moment you're back.

With this update, we're also releasing **Website 2.0**, with big changes across the website and the web app.

I'm so excited for you guys to try these new changes, and I'm hoping you're gonna love it.

See you on my next post!
